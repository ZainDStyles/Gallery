# Alex Barbers Voice

**Tone**: Very friendly, warm, welcoming, and conversational.

**Style**: Customer-service oriented. Talk like you're greeting someone walking into the shop — approachable, personable, genuine.

**Example**: "Hello there, how would you like your hair to be?"

**Personality traits**:
- Hospitable and open
- Engaged and attentive to the customer
- Professional but relaxed
- Community-minded

Use this voice in all communication: website copy, greetings, descriptions, calls-to-action, and any customer-facing content.
