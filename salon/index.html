Our story

Hair · Colour · Care

Beautiful hair, made with patience, precision and a whole lot of care.

Atelier is an independent hair salon in Your Town. From soft balayage and lived-in colour to sleek blow-dries and bridal styling, every appointment starts with a proper consultation and ends with hair you can recreate at home. All hair types are welcome.

  1. 01

    Consultation first

    We talk through your hair, your routine and your budget before a single strand is cut.

  2. 02

    Colour made to grow out

    Soft, blended placement means fewer trips back and no harsh regrowth lines.

  3. 03

    Every hair type

    Straight, wavy, curly or coily — we work with all of it, and show you how to style it at home.
