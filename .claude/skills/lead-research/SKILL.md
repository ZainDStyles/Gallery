# Lead research for TSUGI

## Where to look
- Wealthier / high-footfall areas first. North East: Jesmond, Gosforth, Ponteland & Darras Hall, Whitley Bay, Tynemouth, Morpeth, Newcastle city centre/Quayside. Scotland: Edinburgh New Town, Stockbridge, Morningside, Bruntsfield.
- Sources: Google Maps listings, Yell, Fresha/Booksy/Treatwell, Facebook, Instagram, Checkatrade.

## A good target
- Active: reviews or posts in the last few months, rating 4.3+.
- Weak online: no website, broken site (dead domain, SSL error, parked page), clearly dated / not mobile-friendly, only a social page, or no online booking.
- Not a chain or franchise. Not an existing client or near one (Alex Barbers, Tynemouth). Not already in Tomo's TSUGI Prospects.

## Record per business
Name · category · area + address · rating + review count · website status with evidence (what you saw, when) · every contact channel with link · best channel and why · 1-2 lines on why they're a good fit · the message · checklist of anything unverified.

## Best contact channel
1. Public business email → email.  2. Active Instagram, no email → Instagram DM.  3. Facebook page only → Facebook message.
4. WhatsApp Business number → WhatsApp only if they advertise it for enquiries. Don't suggest cold calls.

## Message rules
- From Zain at TSUGI, local ("I'm based in Newcastle"). DM 50-80 words; email: subject + 90-140 words.
- Specific to what's actually missing for them. Offer to show a free demo link (pick the matching demo from the Demo Links list).
- Never quote prices, never invent results or past clients, no hype.
- Email: end with "If it's not for you, just say and I won't message again." (UK PECR: sole traders count as individuals, so keep it relevant, honest and easy to opt out of.)
- Never send anything yourself. Zain sends, or Tomo puts a Gmail draft in his outbox.

## After
Add each lead to Tomo's TSUGI Prospects (`prospect_save`) so follow-ups and replies are tracked in one place.
