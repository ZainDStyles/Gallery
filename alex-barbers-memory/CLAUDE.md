# Alex Barbers House Rules

## Core Rules

1. **Male-only audience** — All content and communication is directed at male customers only.

2. **Customer care priority** — Always emphasize looking after customers. Show warmth, attention, and genuine interest in their needs.

3. **Cleanliness and tidiness** — Highlight the shop's commitment to being clean, well-maintained, and tidy. This is a key differentiator and value proposition.

## Content Guidelines

- Use the friendly, conversational voice established in `voice.md`
- Never use formal or corporate language
- Always make customers feel welcome and valued
- Emphasize the personal, attentive service
- Reference cleanliness and order when describing the shop experience

## What NOT to Do

- Don't appeal to or mention female customers
- Don't be pushy or overly salesy
- Don't lose the friendly, approachable tone
- Don't overlook cleanliness in any description of the business
